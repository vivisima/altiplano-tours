Uyuni Salt Flat Expedition

Cross the world's largest salt flat — 10,000 km² of blinding white crust at 3,656 m altitude. Visit the steaming Sol de Mañana geysers, swim in the Polques hot springs, and marvel at the flamingo-filled Laguna Colorada. At night, camp under skies with zero light pollution.

  • Transport from Uyuni town
  • English-speaking guide
  • All meals during the tour
  • Accommodation (lodge + camping)
  • National park entry fees

Tiwanaku & Titicaca

Begin at Tiwanaku, a UNESCO World Heritage pre-Inca city that flourished over 1,500 years ago — walk among its monolithic stone gateways and sunken temple. Then head to Lake Titicaca, the world's highest navigable lake, and sail to Isla del Sol and the traditional Uros floating reed islands.

  • Private transport throughout
  • Expert archaeologist guide at Tiwanaku
  • Boat crossing on Lake Titicaca
  • Overnight on Isla del Sol (guesthouse)
  • Breakfast and dinner included

Amazon Wildlife Trek

Venture deep into the Bolivian Amazon — one of the most biodiverse regions on earth. Spot caimans, pink river dolphins, giant otters, and hundreds of bird species with an expert naturalist guide. Night walks, dugout canoe trips, and jungle camp stays bring you face to face with a living rainforest.

  • Return flights La Paz → Rurrenabaque
  • Naturalist guide (English/Spanish)
  • All meals and accommodation
  • Canoe excursions and night walks
  • Madidi National Park entry fee

Cordillera Real Trek

A classic multi-day high-altitude traverse of Bolivia's most dramatic mountain range. Cross glaciated passes above 5,000 m, descend into lush cloud forest valleys, and follow ancient Inca trade routes. Suitable for fit hikers with some trekking experience — acclimatization days in La Paz are recommended before departure.

  • Mule support for gear
  • All camping equipment provided
  • All meals on the trail
  • Emergency satellite communication device

La Paz City & Valle de la Luna

Explore the world's highest administrative capital — its bustling Witches' Market, colonial architecture, and panoramic cable car (Mi Teleférico). In the afternoon, visit Valle de la Luna, where millennia of erosion have sculpted a dreamlike landscape of clay spires and labyrinthine canyons just 10 km from the city centre.

Death Road Mountain Bike

Descend the legendary North Yungas Road — once dubbed the world's most dangerous road — on a high-quality mountain bike. Starting at 4,700 m and plunging 3,500 m through cloud forest to subtropical valley, the 64 km ride is a full-day adrenaline experience with stunning scenery at every turn.

  • Full-suspension mountain bike + helmet
  • Professional safety briefing and guide
  • Transport back to La Paz
  • Lunch and drinks at the finish line
  • Action photos included
